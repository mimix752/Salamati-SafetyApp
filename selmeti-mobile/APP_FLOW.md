═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                          KEY INTERACTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SOS ACTIVATION:
   Tap SOS button → Tap again → Tap third time → Confirm → Alert sent

2. DISGUISE MODE:
   Settings → Enable toggle → App becomes calculator
   Calculator → Triple-tap header → Real app revealed

3. CHATBOT:
   Type message → Send → Receive AI response

4. CONTACT PROFESSIONAL:
   Tap professional card → Tap "Contacter" → Choose Call/Email

5. ADD EMERGENCY CONTACT:
   Enter name → Enter phone → Tap "Ajouter" → Contact saved
